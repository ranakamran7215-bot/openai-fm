Raat ka waqt tha…
Khamoshi har taraf chhayi hui thi…
Sirf hawa ki halki si awaaz aur qabron ke darmiyan jalti hui halki roshni…

Ek ladka kabristan ke beech khara tha…
Uske haath mein mobile tha…
Aur aankhon mein aansu…

Woh dheere se bola:
“Tum chale gaye… aur meri duniya ruk gayi.”

Lekin jab usne apni nazar upar uthai…
Usne dekha…
Hazaron qabrein…
Har qabr ek kahani…
Har qabr ek anjaam…

Tab uske dil mein ek baat utri…

Yahan itne log so rahe hain…
Lekin shehar jaag raha hai…
Sadkein bhari hui hain…
Bachche hans rahe hain…
Zindagi chal rahi hai…

Us waqt usse samajh aaya…
Zindagi kisi ke jaane se nahi rukti.

Jo chale gaye…
Unke saath sirf unke amal gaye…

Na paisa…
Na shoharat…
Na rishtay…

Sirf woh jo Allah ke liye kiya gaya…

Ladka zameen par baith gaya…
Aur aansuon ke saath kaha:
“Main zinda hoon…
Aur mujhe mauqa mila hai…”

Mauqa maa baap ki khidmat ka…
Mauqa gunahon se tauba ka…
Mauqa Allah ke qareeb hone ka…

Kyun ke
maut ka waqt muqarrar hai…
magar zindagi ka istemal humare haath mein hai.

Agar aap yeh video dekh rahe hain…
To yaad rakhein…
Aap zinda hain…
Aur yeh Allah ka bohat bada inaam hai…

Is se pehle ke hum bhi sirf ek qabr ban jaayen…
Apni zindagi ko maqsad wala bana lein…
